The Destnly journey / November 2026

50,000+ Downloads.
And We're Just
Getting Started.

More people. More possibilities. More stories waiting to begin.

By Destnly28 November 2026
A milestone

50,000+ downloads, and a sincere thank-you to everyone who has given Destnly a chance.

Every connection begins somewhere.

Sometimes it is a conversation about a favourite song. Sometimes it is a shared interest, an unexpected friendship, or someone who simply understands you.

Destnly was built around that possibility.

Today, we are celebrating an important milestone in our journey: 50,000+ downloads. It is a milestone we are proud of, but it is also a reminder of how much more there is to build.

More than just dating

Meeting someone new should not always begin with the pressure of finding a relationship.

We believe connections can happen naturally through shared interests, conversations, communities and the little things that make us who we are. That is the idea behind Destnly.

We are building a space where dating and social discovery can coexist. A place where you can discover profiles, share how you feel through Daily Vibe, explore a Circle built around something you care about, find common ground or simply have a good conversation.

Shared interests do not decide where a connection must lead. They give people a more natural place to begin.

What matters to us

Authenticity.
Being yourself should never feel like a disadvantage.
Respect.
Everyone deserves to connect at their own pace, without pressure or judgement.
Safety.
Building trust and improving the experience of meeting new people remain important responsibilities for us.
Belonging.
Different interests, backgrounds and personalities make a community more interesting.
Listening.
Every suggestion, every complaint and every piece of honest feedback gives us an opportunity to build something better.

Growing beyond borders

As Destnly grows, we are seeing people discover our platform across India, the Philippines and beyond.

Different cities. Different cultures. Different stories. One shared desire to connect.

We are excited about building a platform that brings people closer while respecting what makes each of them different.

Growth across borders also asks us to keep listening. The ways people meet, communicate and find belonging are personal, and every community teaches us something new.

This is only the beginning

We are continuing to improve Destnly, exploring better ways to help people discover each other, strengthening our communities and working towards a more comfortable and meaningful social experience.

There is still a lot to learn, improve and create.

To everyone who has downloaded Destnly, shared feedback, recommended us to a friend or simply given us a chance — thank you.

More people. More possibilities. More stories.

50,000+ downloads is a milestone. What comes next is a story we are building together.
