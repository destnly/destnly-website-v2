01 / a feeling, in context

A small answer
with more to say.

Profiles can tell people the basics. A Daily Vibe adds what is happening right now: calm, excited, thoughtful, curious, or something in your own words.

That current feeling appears with your profile, giving someone a more natural place to begin than a blank opening line.

02 / how it works

A more natural
way into a conversation.

Daily Vibe does not replace your profile. It adds one useful, human detail to it.

  1. 01

    Choose how you feel.

    Pick a preset feeling or write a short Vibe in your own words.

  2. 02

    Add today’s context.

    Your profile can say more than who you are. It can also show where your head and heart are today.

  3. 03

    Give someone a real opening.

    A current mood can make it easier to respond with care, ask a relevant question, or simply say, “me too.”
