2. Profile Photos & Basic Face Detection

Destnly allows users to upload a profile photograph as part of their dating profile. We may use basic face detection on an uploaded profile photograph to determine whether a visible face is present and to help provide an appropriate profile-photo experience.

What this means

Destnly's basic face detection is used to improve the profile-photo experience. It is not used for facial recognition, facial identification, biometric identification, authentication, or face matching against another person or external database.

The uploaded photograph itself is part of the user's profile and may be displayed to other users in accordance with Destnly's functionality, profile visibility settings, and Terms of Use.

Basic face detection is not used for advertising or marketing profiling.

10. Data Retention & Security

Data is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the Platform, maintain account functionality, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms of Use, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Account & Profile Deletion

When a user deletes their Destnly profile or account:

  • The account deletion process is initiated.
  • The user's profile is removed from active use.
  • Associated profile information and profile photographs are scheduled for deletion.
  • User-generated content associated with the account is also scheduled for deletion, subject to applicable legal or security requirements.
  • Personal information associated with the deleted profile is deleted within 30 days, subject to information that may need to be retained where required by applicable law, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory requirements.

During the deletion period, a deleted profile is not intended to remain available as an active user profile.

Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational security measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
